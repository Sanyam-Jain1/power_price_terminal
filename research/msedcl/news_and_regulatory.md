## Supply constraints
- Koyna: all machines on standstill from 2 Jun (low reservoir); 1,920 MW under "fuel shortage" 1–9 Jun [PRIMARY]; dam full 5 Sep (uniindia).
- June shortfall attributed to ~1,700 MW MAHAGENCO unusable on coal quality (Chandrapur ~700 MW), ~2,000 MW outages at JSW/RattanIndia/Koradi, thin RTM [SECONDARY] — https://powerpeakdigest.com/maharashtra-1850mw-load-shedding-coal-constraints/
- Coal: 17 Sep — 12 of 22 MH plants critical, MAHAGENCO 25.6% of requirement (psuwatch, 18 Sep). 8 Oct — 15 of 20 critical, MAHAGENCO 19%, Tiroda 6%, GMR Warora 2% (psuwatch, 9 Oct). National 8 Oct: 20.62 MT vs 61.17 MT required, 87 of 191 plants critical.
- IPP outages [PRIMARY]: RattanIndia Amravati U5 (270 MW) coal shortage 21 Aug–~27 Sep; JSW Jaigad U1 (300 MW) out from 7 Jun and 6 Jul; VMEPL coal-feeding outages; daily forced outages 900–2,900 MW.
- Uran gas: A0 (120 MW) out 21 Apr–31 Dec; units often "no schedule by buyer, high cost"; U5 fuel-shortage outage 4 Sep. Spot LNG $10.8→$26/MMBtu (Tribune).
- Tarapur U1&2 (160 MW each) out Apr–May.
- Section 11: CGPL Mundra full-capacity directions 1 Apr–30 Jun (issued 22 Mar), extended to 30 Sep (19 Jun) and 31 Dec (28 Sep); 112 captive plants to run at maximum 1 Oct–31 Dec and sell surplus on exchanges (indianinfrastructure, 28 Sep).
