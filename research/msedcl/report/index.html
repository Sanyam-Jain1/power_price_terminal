A day of decisions, hour by hour

Every day MSEDCL repeats the same cycle. The chart shows the average result for 11 September to 25 October.

  1. The day before, by morning. Forecast demand for every quarter-hour of the next day.
  2. Call contracts in merit order. Requisition contracted plants cheapest energy charge first and leave the costliest unscheduled. Must-run sources (nuclear, renewables, hydro within its water budget) come first.
  3. Bid the day-ahead market for the gap. At midday the exchange is cheaper than MSEDCL's own coal, so it buys there and backs its plants down. In the evening the market is capped and rationed, so only a sliver is filled.
  4. Top up in the real-time market through the day, mostly before dawn and in the late-afternoon ramp.
  5. Live with what is left. Any demand still uncovered is either drawn from the grid as a deviation, charged at DSM rates, or cut.

Method, sources and gaps

  • MSEDCL's schedules by seller come from the state load despatch centre's public scheduling feed, which only serves the most recent six or so weeks. They are scheduled quantities, not metered energy.
  • Daily supply, load shedding and outages come from the daily system reports of the Maharashtra State Load Despatch Centre. Reports for 28 Sep and 2 Oct are not published.
  • Central-station schedules and deviation charges come from the Western Regional Power Committee's weekly block-wise files, for Maharashtra as a whole.
  • Prices of contracted power are MERC's approved energy charges for FY2026-27 from its review order of 25 March 2026. Short-term purchases from out-of-state plants are priced at MERC's approved short-term rate because deal prices are not published; the price-basis column marks them.
  • Exchange prices are IEX clearing prices for each block from the Dhruva terminal's own collection.
  • Not published yet: MSEDCL's source-wise actual purchases after February 2026, its exchange-purchase statements after March 2026, and MERC's fuel-adjustment approvals for this year. When they appear, the energy and costs here can be replaced with actuals.
  • Not separated: deviation charges for MSEDCL alone (only one week of the state's own settlement is public) and the regions hit by load shedding.
  • The raw files, parsing code and the full day-by-seller ledger are in the Dhruva repository under research/msedcl.
